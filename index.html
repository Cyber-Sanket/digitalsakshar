Downloading (उतरवणे)

Receiving files, songs, videos, or PDFs from the internet into your computer or phone.

Common Examples:
  • Saving your train e-ticket PDF
  • Downloading an exam hall ticket
  • Downloading WhatsApp photos to phone gallery
⬆️

Uploading (चढवणे)

Sending files, photos, or forms from your device up onto the internet or server.

Common Examples:
  • Submitting an online college admission form
  • Uploading your passport photo for exam KYC
  • Sending a photo to a WhatsApp family group

📶 Wi-Fi & Hotspot Safety (वाय-फाय सुरक्षितता)

1. Home Wi-Fi Password

Always secure your home router with WPA2/WPA3 encryption. Never leave Wi-Fi open without a password.

2. Public Wi-Fi Risks

Free Wi-Fi at stations and cafes can be monitored by hackers. Never do net-banking or UPI transactions on open public Wi-Fi.

3. Mobile Hotspot

When sharing your phone's cellular data with a laptop via Hotspot, set a strong 8-digit passphrase.

Golden Rule of Banking (RBI Guideline):

Banks NEVER ask for OTP, ATM PIN, or CVV. If someone asks for OTP on a call, they are 100% a fraudster. Never share it!

Essential Cyber Defense Checklist (सायबर सुरक्षा मार्गदर्शक)

1. National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930

If you ever lose money in an online fraud, immediately call 1930 (Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting) or lodge a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in within golden hours.

2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Turn on 2FA for WhatsApp, Gmail, and Instagram. Even if someone steals your password, they cannot log in without the second verification code.

3. Never Install Screen Sharing Apps on Unknown Instructions

Never install apps like AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickSupport if an unknown person on a phone call instructs you to do so. They will see your screen and siphon your money!

💻 Hardware vs Software: The Core Difference

Hardware (हार्डवेअर)

Physical parts you can touch and feel. Examples: Keyboard, Monitor, Mouse, Hard Drive, Motherboard.

Software (सॉफ्टवेअर)

Intangible computer programs, apps, and codes that instruct the hardware what to do. Examples: Windows OS, Chrome, Paint, Word.
