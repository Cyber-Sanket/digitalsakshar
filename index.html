Download (डाऊनलोड)

इंटरनेटवरून माहिती आपल्या उपकरणावर घेणे

Definition: Receiving data or files (such as a song, PDF marksheet, or photo) from the internet and saving it permanently to your mobile or computer.

💡 Analogy: Buying groceries from the market and bringing them home to your kitchen refrigerator.
📤

Upload (अपलोड)

आपल्या उपकरणावरील माहिती इंटरनेटवर पाठवणे

Definition: Sending a file or data from your phone/computer up to a remote website (e.g., uploading your resume on a job portal or sending a photo on WhatsApp).

💡 Analogy: Dropping a written letter into the red post office mailbox so it reaches another destination.
📶

Wi-Fi Basics & Public Hotspots (वाय-फाय मार्गदर्शक)

Wi-Fi connects your devices wirelessly to internet routers using radio frequencies. While private home Wi-Fi with strong WPA2/WPA3 passwords is safe, public open Wi-Fi (at railway stations or tea shops) can be intercepted by hackers.

🛡️

Safety Rule for Public Wi-Fi:

Never do online bank transactions, enter UPI PINs, or pay bills over free public Wi-Fi. Always use your mobile carrier data for payments!
